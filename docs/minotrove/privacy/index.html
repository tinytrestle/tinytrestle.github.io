MinoTrove privacy policy

Android · Updated November 21, 2026

TinyTrestle develops MinoTrove. This policy describes the Android game, including its internal test build. You can play without creating a MinoTrove account.

Progress on your device

The game saves your board, scores, challenge progress, unlocks and settings on your phone so you can resume play. Android’s app storage settings let you clear this local progress.

Gameplay analytics

The Android internal test build automatically sends gameplay and performance events to TinyTrestle. In builds that offer optional analytics, collection starts when you choose “Allow gameplay analytics.” You can see which setting applies in “Gameplay analytics & privacy” in the game.

A random installation identifier links sessions on the same installation. We record event times, game version, platform, screens, puzzle attempts, placements and cancellations, scores, purchases with game coins, reward choices, and frame timing. Tests can also record the board and pieces, refill decisions, and which variation you received. We use these records to find control and performance problems, tune puzzles, and measure whether players return.

First-party gameplay analytics excludes names, email addresses, phone contacts, raw touch paths and advertising IDs. Google Cloud hosts the analytics service for TinyTrestle. The app sends events over HTTPS and authenticates requests using an installation credential. Network services process IP addresses to handle requests.

Daily puzzle reminders

If you enable reminders, the game schedules notifications on your phone for your chosen time. You can turn them off in Daily reminders or in Android’s notification settings. When gameplay analytics is active, we record reminder choices, permission status, test assignment, scheduled times, cancellations, taps, and later gameplay to measure the effect of reminders. Scheduling takes place on your device without a remote push token.

Rewarded ads and Google

MinoTrove includes Google AdMob for optional rewarded ads. Its SDK can request an ad before you choose to watch one. Google’s SDK can collect and share IP addresses (used to estimate general location), app interactions such as launches and ad views, diagnostic information, and identifiers such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID. Google uses this information for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Ad requests use encrypted connections. These SDK practices also matter when the game uses test ads.

Starting with Android version 0.1.9, optional ads use an age range you can choose in the shop or under “Ad privacy options.” The game saves the range on your device. You can skip it and keep playing. Ad requests stay off until an eligible range is selected, and for players who select “Under 13” or “Prefer not to say.” You can change the choice on this device.

TinyTrestle excludes the age range from its gameplay analytics. To request an ad, the game sends Google age-related treatment signals. For ages 13–17, the game requests restricted ads, disables ad personalization and requests restricted data processing. Ages 13–15 receive the stronger under-age-of-consent treatment. The game requests ads rated for general audiences across eligible age ranges.

In applicable regions Google provides consent choices or an option to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. Where available, open “Ad privacy options” under “Gameplay analytics & privacy” to review your ad choices. Google describes its processing, controls and retention in its privacy policy and Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure.

How long we keep data

Raw gameplay events have a 90-day retention window. Inactive installation profiles expire 90 days after their last activity. Temporary event staging expires after seven days, or one day after export. Deletion records are retained for up to 180 days to keep removed data out of reports. Physical cleanup can occur after expiry. Local game saves remain until you remove them.

Contact and deletion

For questions, access requests or deletion requests, email admin@tinytrestle.com. If you contact us, we use your reply address and the information you provide to respond and handle the request. See how to request deletion of MinoTrove data for the steps that apply to your build.
