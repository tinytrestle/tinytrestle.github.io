Delete MinoTrove data

TinyTrestle · Android

MinoTrove works without a player account. Your phone holds your saved game. When analytics is active, TinyTrestle also holds records linked to a random installation identifier.

Request deletion from TinyTrestle

Email admin@tinytrestle.com with the subject “MinoTrove data deletion.” Tell us whether you used the internal test build and whether you still have the installation. Contact us before clearing your phone’s app data if you also want server records removed.

Your email address alone cannot identify a gameplay installation. We may need to follow up to verify which records belong to you. Keep passwords, advertising IDs, authentication tokens and copies of your game files out of the request.

If your build offers optional analytics

Open “Gameplay analytics & privacy” and choose “Turn off & erase analytics.” This clears queued gameplay events and requests erasure of the installation’s server analytics. Keep the installation and reconnect to the internet so the request can reach TinyTrestle. The internal test build uses the email request above.

What gets removed

For a verified installation, erasure covers linked first-party analytics events and removes the installation from active reporting. A deletion record is kept for up to 180 days to prevent the records from reappearing. Processing and physical cleanup take time. See the privacy policy for standard retention periods and Google’s separate ad data practices.

Clear saved progress on your phone

In Android Settings, open Apps, choose MinoTrove, then open Storage and choose Clear storage or Clear data. This removes local scores, progress and preferences. Complete any in-game analytics erasure request before taking this step. You can switch off notifications separately and keep your progress.
